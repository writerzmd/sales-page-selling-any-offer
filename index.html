Before you book

The 6 things callers ask us first

Who exactly will I talk to?

A licensing advisor — a staff member who knows every state's pre-licensing requirements inside out. You won't talk to a sales script or a chatbot.

Is the call really free? What's the catch?

The call is free. We'd rather spend 15 minutes making sure you buy the right course than have you buy the wrong one. If no course fits, we say so and point you elsewhere.

What happens on the call?

Fifteen focused minutes: your state's requirements, which license line matches your goals, and a course-and-exam timeline priced out for you. In the end, you decide.

How long will it take me to get licensed?

Many students complete their pre-licensing course in under two weeks. State requirements, exam scheduling, and results add a few weeks — your advisor will give you a realistic date range for your state.

What if I'm not ready to start yet?

"Just exploring" is fine — that's the most common answer. You'll still leave with a plan and a future timeline; you just won't be enrolled in anything.

What's the exam actually like?

State exams are serious: national first-try pass rates average around 55–60%. That's precisely why the call includes an exam-prep plan — prepared students are literally changing the odds.
